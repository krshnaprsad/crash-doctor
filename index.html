Game Crash Doctor

Paste a crash log or error. Get the likely cause and what to try.

v1.0.0

Paste your crash info

A game can't be fixed automatically, every game breaks differently. This reads what you paste, matches it against a list of known crash causes, and gives you the steps people use to fix them. If it does not recognise the crash, it says so.
