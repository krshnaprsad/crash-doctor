Paste your crash info, or upload a photo of it

Paste a crash report or error from a game, an app or Windows itself and get the likely cause and what to try. A crash can't be fixed automatically, every program breaks differently. This reads what you paste, matches it against a list of known crash causes, and gives you the steps people use to fix them. If it does not recognise the crash, it says so.
